CHECK BEFORE CLASS

AI makes mistakes. The teacher checks.

1. Is the science correct? Test a case with a known answer.
2. Do the numbers and units make sense?
3. Does it work on a phone?
4. What does the student do? Only watch, or also act?

Example of a known answer: rice on a grid, 100 grains on 100 squares. The mean is 1 grain per square, and the Poisson model says about 37 squares stay empty.
