Who's who

    Key idea

    Innate defenseFast. Attacks every germ the same way. Learns nothing.
    Adaptive defenseSlow the first time. Made for one germ only. Remembers it.

    Investigate

    1. Predict first

      Before you press Infect, guess what the number of germs will do in the first 3 days. Then infect at Medium strength and watch.

    2. Find the turning point

      On what day does the germ count start to fall? Which cells show up around that time?

    3. Get sick again

      After the infection clears, press Reinfect. Compare the new curve with the faded one. What changed, and which cells explain it?

    4. Test a vaccine

      Press Reset, tick “Vaccinated before first infection”, then Infect. How does it compare with a first infection? Why?

    5. Weak germs, strong germs

      Try Very mild and Very strong germs, pressing Reset between tries. When can the innate defense win on its own? Does that leave memory cells?
